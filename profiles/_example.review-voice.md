# Review voice — example

Copy to `profiles/review-voice.md` and rewrite it as **you** actually write. That file is untracked.

`pr-comments.md` reads it before drafting anything, and **asks rather than inventing a voice** when it is
missing — because a comment posted under your name in a register you do not use reads as you, and you
cannot unsay it. Fill it from comments you have actually written, not from how you think you should write.

The axes below are the ones that change the output. Delete any that do not matter to you; a short honest
file beats a complete invented one.

## Language

Japanese / English / Japanese with English technical terms left in English. Say which, and say whether
it changes by repository or by audience.

## Register

- **Assertion or question.** "これは N+1 になる" versus "ここ N+1 になりませんか？" — most people are not
  uniform about this, so say what decides it. (Severity? Whether you are sure? Whether the author is
  senior to you?)
- **Directness.** Does the finding come first, or the context?
- **Hedging.** Which hedges you actually use, and which read as false modesty when you write them.
- **Length.** One or two sentences per comment? Or the full reasoning inline?

## Conventions you keep

Examples of the kind of thing to record — replace with yours:

- Nits marked how (`Nit:` / `[nit]` / 「些細ですが」), and whether they are batched or separate
- Whether you name the fix or only the problem
- Whether you cite `file:line` for the thing you read, or only for the thing you are commenting on
- What you never do (emoji? praise openers? "just"/「一応」?)
